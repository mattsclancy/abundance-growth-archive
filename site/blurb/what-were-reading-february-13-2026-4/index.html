Repealing the endangerment finding, uncertainty included

EnergyEnvironment & Climate

by Willow Latham-Proenca · about work by Philip Rossetti, Diego Kaenzig

The EPA just repealed the endangerment finding – the 2009 determination that greenhouse gases endanger public health and welfare, and the regulatory foundation for US climate policy under the Clean Air Act. Philip Rossetti at R Street has a helpful take on the legal battles likely to follow, and why sound regulatory design beats legal whipsawing. Timely: a working paper out last month (bite-sized summary here) finds that this type of climate policy uncertainty reverberates through the economy, depressing firms’ investment and R&D, reducing output, and raising prices.
