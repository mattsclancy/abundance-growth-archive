Progressives turn on the build-to-rent ban

Housing Policy

by Alex Armlovich · about work by Urban Institute

More progressives in the housing space are coming out against the Senate’s build-to-rent ban. The growing concern is that the ban could reduce rental housing supply when affordability remains a pressing issue. It’s a tough situation and we hope all stakeholders come to a pro-housing solution with sustainable politics.
