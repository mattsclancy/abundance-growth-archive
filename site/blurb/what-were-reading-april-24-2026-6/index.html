A podcast wishlist for clinical trial reform

Clinical Trials

by Saloni Dattani · about work by Ruxandra Teslo, Ben Southwood

On a new episode of the Works in Progress podcast, Ruxandra Teslo, Ben Southwood and I chatted about everything you wanted to know about clinical trial reform — from why ethics reviews are so inefficient to how Australia has made it simpler and faster to run earlier stage trials. We also discuss our wishlist for policy reforms and some of the worst ideas we’ve heard.
